Qualtrics to LimeSurvey

Move your survey.
Keep its logic.

Turn your Qualtrics survey into a LimeSurvey survey. Your questions, and the rules about who sees what, come along.

Everything stays on your own computer

This page opens in your web browser, but the conversion happens on your computer, not on a server. Your survey file is not uploaded, sent or saved anywhere, and nobody else can see it.

You can check this yourself: once this page has loaded, switch off your internet connection. It keeps working.

  1. 1. Get your file from QualtricsOpen your survey in Qualtrics, click Tools, then Import/Export, then Export survey. A file ending in .qsf is saved on your computer.
  2. 2. Open it hereClick the box above and pick that file. If something needs your choice, this page asks you in plain words.
  3. 3. Put it into LimeSurveyClick Download, then in LimeSurvey choose Create survey, Import, and pick the downloaded file (it ends in .lss).
