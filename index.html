Qualtrics to LimeSurvey

Converts a Qualtrics survey export (.qsf) into a LimeSurvey survey file (.lss), including display and skip logic, survey flow branches, randomizers, embedded data, piped text and loops. Your file never leaves your computer.

In Qualtrics: Survey > Tools > Import/Export > Export survey. In LimeSurvey: Create survey > Import, then choose the .lss file.
